The workflow

One signal. Six decisions.

  1. 01

    Sense

    Capture the customer’s actual words and where they got stuck.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Define the audience, the action you want them to take, and the decision you need to make.

  3. 03

    Research

    Use AI to plan the research. Open the original sources and check what they support.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Write the test and set your pass criteria before you see the results.

  5. 05

    Build and Ship

    Make one reviewable change. Check the mobile path, tracking, and rollback.

  6. 06

    Measure and Compound

    Compare against your baseline and decide whether to keep, revise, or stop.

Revenue and impact

Know where the next marketing dollar should go.

Find the demand you’re missing, choose a test your team can deliver, and measure what changed in sales, qualified leads, or returns. Turn what you learn into a check your team can run again next week.

  • Compare paid and organic search coverage to decide which gap deserves the next dollar or hour.
  • Check inventory, fulfillment, or appointment capacity before spending to drive more demand.
  • When you change product copy, read return rate alongside units sold to see whether you’re protecting revenue.
