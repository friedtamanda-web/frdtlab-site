Setup steps

Take a test order all the way from checkout to fulfillment before you invite customers in.

  1. Open the store. Confirm who owns the Shopify account and who handles payments, inventory, and customer support.
  2. Build a sellable catalog. Confirm product and variant prices, inventory, photos, weights, and availability.
  3. Configure checkout. Set up payments, taxes, shipping and local pickup as applicable. Review contact, returns, and privacy pages.
  4. Test two paths. Place a test order for each enabled fulfillment method. Check the customer confirmation, staff notification, fulfillment steps, and refund workflow. Confirm mobile checkout.
  5. Connect the domain. Confirm the primary URL and SSL, check redirects from any prior site, verify Google Search Console, and update the website URL in your Google Business Profile if applicable.
  6. Go live. Disable test mode, remove the storefront password when ready, and assign someone to watch the first real orders and support messages.

What to verify before removing the storefront password

Have someone place an order on a phone using each fulfillment option you offer. Confirm that inventory updates correctly, the customer receives an order confirmation, and your team knows who handles the first pickup, shipment, or refund.
