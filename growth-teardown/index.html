If your commuter backpack page gets visits but shoppers still can’t tell whether their laptop fits, what do you fix first? This is what the answer looks like in writing.

FRDT.LAB / TEARDOWN NOTESSTOREFRONT · 01
  1. 01

    Answer the fit question first.

    Finding: Laptop dimensions are buried under Specifications.

    Fix: Put the tested fit beside the price. Keep exact internal measurements one tap away.

    Verify: Check the claim against the product spec, then watch add-to-cart rate on this page.

  2. 02

    Show the delivery promise.

    Finding: Shoppers have to start checkout to learn when it ships.

    Fix: Put a reliable shipping window next to the buying controls.

    Verify: Test the estimate against checkout for supported destinations.

  3. 03

    Check the measurement.

    Finding: The product page and checkout numbers don’t reconcile.

    Fix: Check whether a single tap fires a single add-to-cart event.

    Verify: Run one test journey on mobile and inspect each event.

Before you book

A few practical questions.

What is a growth teardown?

It’s a 90-minute ecommerce growth audit focused on one storefront, product feed, or conversion funnel. You leave with three prioritized fixes and a way to measure progress.

Will you look at SEO and AI search?

For the storefront focus, we can look at whether key pages are discoverable and clearly explain the product, along with the practical steps that could improve how search engines and AI search systems understand them. No rankings or mentions are guaranteed.

Do you need access to my accounts?

A public link is enough to start. Screenshots, exports, or a screen share can help if the issue is in Merchant Center or analytics. Don’t send passwords.

How do pricing and scheduling work?

Send the link and the issue you want to solve. FRDT Lab will confirm the scope, price, and available times before you pay. The session is prepaid once the scope and time are agreed.
