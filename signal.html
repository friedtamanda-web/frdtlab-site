The Method

Noise becomes clarity in five moves.

A signal isn't useful until it moves. The lab's job is to carry an observation from raw noise all the way to action, then feed what works back into the loop.

→ NOISE

Capture

Pull raw inputs from search logs, communities, feeds, and behavior.

→ AWARENESS

Detect

Surface the pattern hiding in the volume. Name the real signal.

→ CLARITY

Test

Run the experiment. Prove or kill the hypothesis with evidence.

→ ACTION

Build

Encode the result into a framework, feed, or repeatable system.

→ MOMENTUM

Compound

Turn the finding into content, frameworks, and conversation that compounds over time.
