Where should the next search dollar go?

Pair an SEO and PPC lead. Bring a common date range, market, and device scope from Search Console and Google Ads, plus net sales or qualified lead data from your reporting system. Use the sample when exports or permissions are missing.

1. Sense · 10 min

Pick one theme where a customer need and a business opportunity intersect. Check stock, lead capacity, or margin before promising a test.

2. Structure · 15 min

Normalize query text and tag themes. Aggregate each channel separately before joining by theme, date, and market. Keep brand separate and mark missing rows as unknown.

3. Research · 10 min

Open the actual paid and organic landing pages. Compare likely intent, page fit, and the next action. Put the source link next to each finding.

4. Validate · 15 min

Label what you observed, what you assume, and what would prove or disprove it. Paid-attributed sales show what Ads recorded. Use a comparison group to evaluate incremental impact.

5. Build and Ship · 15 min

Choose one page or spend change. Assign a shared owner, define a comparison, and record the budget cap or margin floor before launch.

6. Measure and Compound · 10 min

Pick one business metric, a review date, and a stop rule. Every week refresh the table, check tracking and inventory, and log whether to keep, revise, or stop.

See the facilitator’s worked example

Start with compatible replacement parts. The sample shows 310 paid clicks and 25 organic clicks, with $1,980 in Ads-attributed sales on $620 spend. The hypothesis is that a clearer compatibility guide on the existing landing page increases net sales per eligible visit. Split eligible visitors between the current and revised page, keep campaign settings stable, and log traffic mix. Review after 28 days and extend if order volume is insufficient. Track margin and compatibility-related returns, allowing for the return window. This test measures the page change across traffic sources. A separate holdout would be needed to estimate incremental ad spend impact.

Live build and weekly operating checklist
  1. Export matched dates, markets, and devices where supported. Keep source links and export dates. Capture omitted queries and conversion lag as limitations.
  2. Normalize and aggregate before joining. Flag unmapped rows and validate totals at each stage.
  3. Set up a scheduled refresh for the inputs your tools allow. Flag stale exports, unmapped themes, tracking changes and inventory shifts for a person to review.
  4. Record the test, owner, comparison, decision and evidence each week. Use Drafted, Tested, Verified, Blocked and Ready to track progress.
