FRDT / SIGNAL BRIEF 8 SLIDES
02 / 08SEO
IGNORE

Mystery August algorithm update panic.

WHY

Ranking movement does not automatically mean there was a confirmed Google update.

FOCUS INSTEAD

Query class, landing pages, SERP layout, AI Overviews, competitors, site changes, and conversion impact.

FRDT / SIGNAL BRIEF
03 / 08SEO
IGNORE

AI Overviews killed SEO.

OLD KPI
Rankings → clicks
NEW KPI
Visibility → influence → acquisition
WHY

AI results change click behavior, especially for informational searches, but search can still create discovery, influence, demand, and revenue.

FOCUS INSTEAD

Measure AI visibility, citations, brand influence, commercial traffic, and conversions separately.

FRDT / SIGNAL BRIEF
04 / 08AI SEARCH
IGNORE

Being cited by AI means your brand is winning.

NOT THE FINISH LINE
WHY

A citation is not the same as a recommendation, preference, trust, or revenue.

FOCUS INSTEAD

Track citation share, recommendation share, factual accuracy, and downstream conversion.

FRDT / SIGNAL BRIEF
05 / 08AI SEARCH
IGNORE

Planting fake Reddit mentions as an AI search strategy.

MANIPULATION
Seeded threads, borrowed voices
INTELLIGENCE
Real participation, real language
WHY

Fake community participation is brand risk pretending to be SEO.

FOCUS INSTEAD

Use Reddit for customer language, objections, product gaps, and real participation.

06 / 08BRAND
IGNORE

Consumers hate AI, so brands should stop using it.

WHY

The issue is not AI itself. The issue is sloppy, hidden, low quality, or unwanted AI.

FOCUS INSTEAD
Consent Provenance Human review Disclosure Quality control

07 / 08PAID MEDIA
IGNORE

This week's Google Ads volatility is just the algorithm.

Target CPA≠Actual CPA
WHY

Bid strategy changes may cause campaigns to follow old CPA and ROAS targets more literally.

FOCUS INSTEAD

Audit limited-by-budget campaigns and compare actual performance against configured targets.
