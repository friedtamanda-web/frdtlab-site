The short version

What does an AI search consultant actually do?

An AI search consultant makes your brand easy for AI systems to read, trust, and recommend. Below: what the work includes, how it relates to the SEO you already have, and how to choose the right person for it.

  • AI search optimization. Fix the signals ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews use to decide who to name: entity consistency, structured data, product and brand facts, and third-party corroboration.
  • AI visibility tracking. A repeatable prompt set run against your category, so you can see where you're mentioned, where you're cited, and where a competitor is named instead.
  • Content that answers the real question. Pages built around what buyers ask an assistant, not just the keywords they type into Google.
  • An SEO strategy that still holds. Traditional SEO is the foundation AI search builds on. The work extends it, it doesn't throw it out.
  • Measurement you can defend. Visibility, citations, and share of voice tracked over time, tied back to traffic and revenue where the data allows.

AI search consultant, SEO consultant, or SEO agency?

  • SEO consultant or SEO agency: earns rankings in a list of ten links. Still necessary, and still the base layer.
  • AI SEO consultant / AI search consultant: earns the recommendation inside a single AI answer. Works on how machines understand your brand, not only where a page ranks.
  • The overlap: clean technical SEO, structured data, and authority help both. A good AI search consultant should work with your existing SEO team, not around it.

The Shift

The category recommendation moved from search results to a single answer.

A customer used to type a query, scan ten links, and pick. Now they ask an assistant "who's best for this," and it hands back one or two names. If you're not one of them, you don't lose position three. You lose the conversation entirely.

These systems don't rank you. They decide whether they trust you. That trust is built from signals: consistent identity across the web, clean structured data, third-party corroboration, and content that answers the real question. Miss those and the best product in the market stays invisible.

Most brands don't have a visibility problem. They have a legibility problem. The machine can't read them clearly enough to risk recommending them.

How to choose

Five questions to ask any AI search consultant.

  • "Show me where we're cited today." A real consultant starts with a live check across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI, not a generic audit template.
  • "How will you measure it?" Ask for the prompt set, the platforms covered, and how often it's re-run. If the answer is "rankings," that's SEO, not AI search.
  • "What will you change first, and why?" You want a short list ordered by impact, not a hundred tasks.
  • "How does this work with our SEO team?" The right answer builds on what you have.
  • "What have you moved for someone else?" Ask for numbers. Mine: one catalog went from 28.51% to 83.43% search visibility in a single quarter.

What does an AI search consultant actually do?

Makes your brand legible and trustworthy to AI systems so they recommend you when someone asks a question in your category. That means fixing entity consistency, structured data, corroborated authority, and content that answers the real question, then measuring where you show up across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI.

How do I choose an AI search consultant?

Ask them to show you where you're cited today, how they'll measure progress, what they'll change first, how they'll work with your SEO team, and what they've moved for someone else. Specific answers beat a polished deck.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO earns a ranking. GEO earns a recommendation. AI systems don't hand back ten links; they give one answer and maybe two names. GEO builds the confidence signals that put you in that answer. It sits on top of solid SEO, it doesn't replace it.
