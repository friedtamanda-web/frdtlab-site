The Shift

The category recommendation moved from search results to a single answer.

A customer used to type a query, scan ten links, and pick. Now they ask an assistant "who's best for this," and it hands back one or two names. If you're not one of them, you don't lose position three. You lose the conversation entirely.

These systems don't rank you. They decide whether they trust you. That trust is built from signals: consistent identity across the web, clean structured data, third-party corroboration, and content that answers the real question. Miss those and the best product in the market stays invisible.

Most brands don't have a visibility problem. They have a legibility problem. The machine can't read them clearly enough to risk recommending them.

FAQ

What does an AI search consultant actually do?

Makes your brand legible and trustworthy to AI systems so they recommend you when someone asks a question in your category. That means fixing entity consistency, structured data, corroborated authority, and content that answers the real question, then measuring where you show up across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO earns a ranking. GEO earns a recommendation. AI systems don't hand back ten links; they give one answer and maybe two names. GEO builds the confidence signals that put you in that answer. It sits on top of solid SEO, it doesn't replace it.
