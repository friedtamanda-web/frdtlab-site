How I work

Clarity first, then momentum.

Look before you spend. Find the few moves that matter. Build it to run without you.

You don’t have to bet the budget to find out what works. Most teams I meet are stuck in the same place. Organic traffic is slipping. The ads aren’t as efficient as they used to be. They know they should be doing more with AI and have no idea where to start, and they don’t want to put out a pile of AI slop with their name on it. They know video and social matter too, but the time has to come from somewhere. Every option feels expensive and nothing feels certain.

Here’s what’s usually going on. The setup is outdated. The website, the Merchant Center, the ad accounts, the social, the data underneath it. None of it is broken. It just hasn’t been upgraded to keep up. That’s good news, because it means we don’t have to reinvent the wheel. Sometimes a rebuild is the right call. More often there’s a lot of performance sitting in what you already have.

So we start with the real questions. Where do you start? What are the three moves that matter? What do you actually do this week, this month? And when it’s time to launch something new, what’s the path of least resistance to get out the gate without burning months of time?

It’s not just about spending money smarter. It’s about spending your time and resources smarter too.

Sometimes the question is about your people. Do I have the right team at the table? Is there a skills gap? That’s fixable. I can put the tools, training, and processes in place so your team grows into it. I learned that on the agency side. You don’t have to come in and take everything over. You come in, install what’s missing, and hand it back working.

And this isn’t only marketing. It’s marketing, revenue, and operations, plus the systems and technology underneath all three. I’ve installed EOS operating systems, built standard operating procedures, and set up the processes teams need to actually run. I’ve done it for small businesses, seed and early-stage companies, Series A startups, and global organizations integrating teams across the world.

As we build, I run test and learn. I make a set of changes to campaigns or pages, give it about seven days, and read what the data actually says. The wins feed back into the system, and then we install the next batch. You’re not guessing, and you’re not betting everything on one big swing.

Sometimes you just want a second read. Is my agency doing this right? Is my team? Is there something we’re missing? I’ll tell you straight, and I’ll do it without trashing anybody. Most teams are doing solid work. It usually just needs sharpening with what’s current.

That’s the part I can promise. I’ve spent my whole career keeping up with this industry, and I still build every day. New websites, new campaigns, new agentic systems. When I tell you what’s worth doing, it’s because I’m doing it right now.

Case · E-commerce catalog

A demand drop that wasn’t

  • Looked like organic demand had fallen for 16 months. It hadn’t. The signal path had collapsed: feed disapprovals, product descriptions that didn’t match how people searched, a flat structure eating its own traffic.
  • We rebuilt the product titles around what the buyer was actually carrying, fixed the feed, and gave the catalog a structure that could be found.
  • Products that ranked for nothing started pulling real search traffic. Feed disapprovals fell from roughly 43 percent to clean.
