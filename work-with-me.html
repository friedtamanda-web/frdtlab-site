How I work

Clarity first. Then momentum.

Most teams I meet do not have a marketing problem. They have a clarity problem. Three departments are quietly optimizing three different versions of the same goal, and nobody is solving the same one.

So we start upstream. We find the friction, name the real issue, and build the system that produces the outcome instead of chasing it. Structure creates freedom. Once the wiring is right, the work starts to compound on its own.

The engagements that work best are collaborative, honest, and a little uncomfortable in the good way. If that sounds like what you need, let's talk.
