What is FRDTLAB

FRDT Lab is where growth strategy becomes infrastructure.

Most companies have plenty of data, platforms, campaigns, and dashboards. What they lack is a system that connects them.

I work across search, paid media, content, analytics, customer behavior, merchandising, technology, and operations to find the signals that matter, validate them across independent sources, and turn them into decisions teams can act on. Cross-validation prevents expensive decisions based on incomplete evidence.

The work combines nearly 20 years of growth marketing and operating experience with modern AI, automation, and agentic systems.
