Questions

Integrated marketing, answered.

What does an integrated marketing consultant do?

An integrated marketing consultant connects the parts of your marketing that usually run separately: search, paid media, content, email, your website, and reporting. The goal is one strategy where every channel works from the same customer and revenue data, so campaigns reinforce each other instead of competing for budget and credit.

What is integrated marketing?

Integrated marketing is coordinating all of your marketing communications so they work as one system: one message, one plan, and one set of data across every channel. In the AI search era it also means making sure the same facts about your brand show up consistently everywhere machines look.

How is working with a consultant different from hiring an agency?

An agency typically sells execution hours across channels. Here you work directly with the operator who builds the system, alongside your team, so the strategy and the measurement stay with you after the engagement ends.

Where does AI search fit into integrated marketing?

AI search is now one of the channels, and it depends on all the others. ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews recommend brands they can read clearly and see corroborated across the web. Integrated marketing is how you give them that consistency.

How do I choose an integrated marketing consultant?

Ask to see results tied to revenue, how they measure across channels, what they would change first, and how they work with your existing team or agency. Specific answers beat a polished deck.
