FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is an AI visibility audit?

It tests how AI answer engines see, describe, and recommend your brand. It runs the real questions your buyers ask, documents where you appear and where you're missing, and returns a scored report with prioritized fixes.

How is this different from an SEO audit?

An SEO audit examines how search engines rank your pages. This examines how language models understand your brand as an entity and whether they recommend it with confidence. Strong SEO does not guarantee AI visibility.

How long does it take?

One week from intake to delivered report. The readout call on Tier 02 gets scheduled at delivery.
