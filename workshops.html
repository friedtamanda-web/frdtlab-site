The New SEO · AI Search

Where Your Customers Actually Look Now

Search is not just Google anymore. The same person, with the same question, now asks ChatGPT, scrolls TikTok, checks Reddit, and watches a YouTube review before they ever land on your site.

This is the plain-English session on how people find and choose you today, and why showing up in one place is no longer enough. We cover what changed, where your buyers actually search now, and the difference between ranking on Google and getting recommended by an AI. Social platforms have quietly become search engines, and most brands have not caught up.

  • A clear map of every place your customers search, not just Google
  • Why AI recommends some brands and ignores others, in simple terms
  • The first three fixes to be findable everywhere they look
  • How your social content doubles as search

Founders, marketing teams, and anyone who owns the number but doesn't live in SEO tools.

Agentic Marketing

Let the System Do the Heavy Lifting

You don't need more tools or more hours. You need a workflow where one piece of research turns into a post, a page, an email, an ad, and an answer an AI can find, all from the same work.

This is a working session on building simple AI-assisted workflows that connect the work you're already doing. No engineering degree required. We walk through a loop anyone can run: notice what's changing, organize it, ship it across channels, measure it, and let each round make the next one easier. You leave with one workflow you can run next week, and a clear sense of where AI actually helps versus where it just wastes your time.

  • The sense, structure, ship, measure loop in plain terms
  • One repeatable workflow your team can run immediately
  • How one idea becomes a week of social content
  • Where AI helps and where it quietly hurts

Best for

Content Velocity

Turn Content Into a Growth Engine

One post is content. Volume is infrastructure. Done with a little structure, your content trains the algorithm, trains the AI, and trains your audience, all at the same time.

This is how to make social content at volume without burning out, and why quantity done right stops being noise and becomes an asset. We focus on short-form video and social, the format where most growth is happening now, and the simple system that keeps it from feeling like a treadmill. You leave understanding how volume compounds into reach, search signal, and authority instead of just more posting.

  • A repeatable system for short-form video and social
  • Why volume, done with structure, compounds into reach
  • How your content becomes search signal and AI training at once
  • How to keep the pace without burning the team out
