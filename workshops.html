Let the System Do the Heavy Lifting

Half-day hands-on sessions are the most common booking. Request a quote through the form and you'll get a recommended format and a firm number back.

Are these workshops remote or in person?

Both. Remote runs fine for intros and half-days; full-day intensives are strongest in person. Detroit-based, travels anywhere.

What level does my team need to be?

Any. Every session runs at two levels, foundational or applied, and the depth is set when you book, not discovered in the room.
