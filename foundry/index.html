Questions

Who actually does the work?

FRDT Foundry is a build program of FRDT LAB. Strategy, direction, and accountability sit here in Detroit with Amanda. The engineering is handled by our senior build team — the same crew behind 150+ custom sites over 17 years. One contract, one invoice, one person answerable to you.

Do I own the website?

Completely. Domain, code, content, tracking data — all of it is yours from day one. If we ever part ways, everything keeps working and everything stays with you. That's the difference between infrastructure and a lease.

What does it cost?

Every shop's situation is different — a two-truck electrician and a forty-truck HVAC operation don't need the same machine. So every engagement starts with the teardown: a $750 hand-built audit, delivered in writing, that scopes exactly what your business needs. If you move forward, the full $750 is credited toward your build — so serious buyers pay nothing extra for it. No rate card. No surprises.

How long does a build take?

Six to eight weeks for most home services sites, from kickoff to launch. Larger multi-trade builds run longer. You'll have a real timeline in writing before anything starts.

Why not one of the big home-services marketing platforms?

They're good at scale and bad at ownership. Their tracking numbers, landing pages, and dashboards live on their infrastructure — cancel and the equity walks out with them. We build the opposite way: everything on your infrastructure, compounding for you. You should be the one who owns the machine.

Is my current site salvageable?

Sometimes, yes. The teardown will tell you honestly. If a rebuild isn't warranted, we'll say so and show you the smaller fixes that matter. We'd rather be right than be hired.
