How an engagement starts

What the first 30 days look like.

The first month fixes the signal. Nothing scales until the gate in front of it is met, so you never pay to amplify bad data.

Weeks 1 to 4 · The first 30 days

Fix the signal

  • Rebuild conversion goals
  • End double counting
  • Resolve duplicate tracking tags
  • Negatives, locations, and offer terms

Gate: conversions verified, definitions signed off

→
Weeks 5 to 8

Close the loop

  • Closed deals sent back to the ad platforms
  • Enhanced conversions
  • Social ads audit and server-side conversions
  • First revenue report

Gate: outcomes flowing back at an agreed match rate

→
Month 3 onward

Grow

  • Bid to revenue, not lead count
  • Landing page and offer tests
  • Fresh creative every two weeks
  • A seasonal plan

Measured by: revenue per closed deal

Every change in every phase is logged with the rule that triggered it, and anything customer-facing waits for your approval.
