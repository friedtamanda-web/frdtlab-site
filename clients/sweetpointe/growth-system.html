The Website

A smaller site.
Every foundation.

A few pages, done right — not a sprawling rebuild you'll never finish updating. And we're not moving you off what already works.

You run Lightspeed. Your register, your inventory, your day live there. Re-platforming to Shopify means abandoning that or running two systems that fight each other — cost and risk a young shop doesn't need. So we don't migrate you. We build a small, fast, conversion-first site that plugs into Lightspeed and leaves it as your single source of truth. One place to update. No double entry.

Built on what you have

Lightspeed stays the source of truth; the site syncs to it. No migration, nothing for your team to relearn.

Small, but complete

Home plus the pages that make money — parties, gift boxes, gifting. Each one on the full foundation: local schema, tracking, mobile + speed, AI-findable.

An easy button for your team

We build it with an agentic workflow and hand you a simple UI. Change a price, swap a photo, post a box — no developer, no ticket.

The site should be the easiest thing you touch all week — not the scariest.

Social Support

A month of content.

We don't run your social forever. We build the machine, prove it, and hand you the keys.

The Machine Around The Site

Pages don't sell.
Flows do.

The site gets you found. The flows and content around it turn a visit into a booking, a box, or a gift order — and bring people back without you lifting a finger.

Money flows

Party booking, gift-box order, and a corporate/realtor gifting inquiry → quote — plus a "which box is right?" gift-finder that lifts the average order.

Email that runs itself

A welcome series built on your 1932 story, a birthday club that auto-sends a treat that month, and seasonal sends timed to the calendar that already spikes.

Findable content

An FAQ hub written so AI lifts it word-for-word, the heritage story, and light gift guides that catch "teacher gifts under $30" and "retro candy michigan."

A reviews engine

The single biggest lever for a place people drive to. We set up the ask, the response rhythm, and push your best reviews out across the site and your Google profile — not buried on one page.

An agentic marketing layer

The part that keeps working after launch: workflows that draft posts, refresh gift guides, and flag SEO and schema gaps. You approve, they run — built on the tools you already pay for, not another subscription.

Numbers you can see

GA4, Search Console, and social insights wired into one Looker dashboard on the four things that matter — party bookings, box orders, gifting inquiries, list signups. No vanity metrics.
