SCOPE GUARDRAILS
  • The fee covers the scoped update, not unlimited builds. New systems, migrations, full e-commerce rebuilds, or major campaigns are scoped as separate batched builds.
  • Existing platform, refreshed. The update happens on your current platform (or an agreed equivalent) — not an open-ended custom rebuild.
  • Goal windows, not fixed deadlines. Launch targets are steered toward; timelines shift with content readiness and access. Milestones gate the work, not calendar dates.
  • A defined cadence. Onboarding session plus weekly check-ins during the support window — not open-ended on-demand availability.
  • Client provides timely access & assets. Domain, logins, GBP access, confirmed hours, and real store + product photos (phone photos work — shot list provided at onboarding). Milestone clocks pause when access, assets, or sign-off is the blocker.
  • Tool fees + paid media are separate. Subscriptions billed to client. Paid advertising is a distinct managed budget with its own scope.
