The Shift

Search used to rank you. AI answers recommend you.

FAQ

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the work of making your brand visible and citable to AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Instead of optimizing for a ranked list of links, you optimize for being the answer an AI recommends with confidence.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. GEO builds on the same foundation. Clean structure, clear entities, and trustworthy signals feed both traditional search and AI answers. The playbook shows you where the two overlap and where AI answers need something different.
