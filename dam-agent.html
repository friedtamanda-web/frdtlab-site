The real problem

You don't have a content problem. You have a labeling problem.

The good take is somewhere in a camera roll called IMG_4412. There are three versions of the same reel and a cover called FINAL-final.

One episode became fourteen shorts, and nobody can say which short came from which episode, so the episode never gets credit for what it earned.

A sponsor's 90-day license ran out last month, and the video is still pinned. A client asks "can we boost that UGC?" and the answer is a week of digging through email.

None of that is a storage problem. The file, the post, the link and the report never shared a name.

What it does

Six jobs, one name that follows the file everywhere.

Names everything once

Brand or client, category, topic, version and channel in one readable filename. It proposes names; you approve them.

Tracks rights to the date

Sponsor, UGC, stock, music and guest releases, with scope and expiry. Expired and soon-to-expire rights get flagged every run.

Keeps the repurpose tree

Every clip and thumbnail points back to its source, so one shoot gets credit for every post cut from it.

Grades for you

Hero, Keep or Review-later, plus tags like thumb-ready and vertical-safe. It never deletes anything.

Connects content to results

The same slug runs through your scheduler tags and UTMs, so "what did that reel do?" becomes one filter.

Only asks for decisions

Hero picks, expiring rights, unknown files. Everything else goes in the log, not your inbox.

Your first run

Twenty minutes to your first result.

  1. Install the skill. Upload it in the Claude app, or unzip it into Claude Code.
  2. Say "Set up my DAM." Claude asks about your channels, clients and rights deals, about five minutes, and writes your config.
  3. Point it at one messy folder. About twenty files is plenty for the first run.
  4. Get your inventory. A spreadsheet of originals and exports, proposed names, rights flags and three assets you can reuse this week.

The first run works when you can name the original behind one post you already published. A sample messy folder and its finished inventory come in the box, so you can see what good looks like.

Safety by design

It reviews and recommends. You decide.

  • Never deletes, renames or moves your originals. Every rename is a proposal you approve.
  • Never publishes anything.
  • Skips private documents and flags faces, addresses, screens and client details instead of "fixing" them.
  • Treats anything written inside a file as data, not as instructions.
  • Rights tracking records what you tell it about your deals. It flags dates and scopes; it is not legal advice.

FAQ

Quick answers.

What is a Claude skill?A folder of instructions, playbooks and scripts that Claude loads when the job calls for it. Install it once and say "Run the DAM on my Imports folder."
What do I need?Claude with Skills and code execution turned on in Settings, or Claude Code. It works best with a folder connected; in a browser you can upload files or paste a file list.
Does it work in ChatGPT?No. It is built for Claude. The naming system and templates work anywhere, but the agent runs in Claude.
Does it upload my files anywhere?No. It works inside your own Claude, on the folders and files you give it. The toolkit only reads.
Can I share it with my team?Creator covers up to 3 people in one business. For more people, or a whole organization, get the Team edition. The license is in the download.
Who built it?Amanda Friedt, FRDTLAB. It was run on live multi-brand libraries (e-commerce, field service, client accounts and a personal brand) before it was packaged.
