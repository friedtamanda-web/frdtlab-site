Your first win

Product Feed Ownership Audit

Choose Yes, No, or Not sure for each. “Not sure” is useful information, too.

Eight controls · about five minutes

01 / OWNERSHIP

One person owns the feed

Is there a named owner who can fix product data, coordinate the store and feed, and approve changes?

Evidence: owner, backup, and escalation path
02 / INVENTORY

You know what is eligible

Can you identify approved, limited, and disapproved products in Merchant Center and explain the main reasons?

Evidence: dated diagnostics or item status report
03 / PRODUCT TRUTH

Attributes match the actual product

Are titles, identifiers, dimensions, variants, and compatibility claims accurate, supported, and consistently mapped?

Evidence: source-of-truth fields and sample SKU checks
04 / VISUALS

Images represent the right items

Do primary images match the listed variants and satisfy the applicable Merchant Center image requirements?

Evidence: item-to-image mapping and diagnostics
05 / PRICE & STOCK

Feed and storefront agree

Are price, availability, and landing-page details consistent, including sale and variant behavior?

Evidence: recent spot-checks and sync owner
06 / CUSTOMER FIT

Shoppers can choose correctly

Does the product page explain fit, compatibility, included parts, and important constraints before checkout?

Evidence: support themes, return reasons, page copy
07 / CHANGE CONTROL

Updates have a QA gate

Can you trace who changed the catalog, what changed, when it changed, and whether the feed was rechecked?

Evidence: change log, QA signoff, reprocessing check
08 / MONITORING

Someone knows when it breaks

Is there a routine to review material eligibility changes and route issues to someone who can act?

Evidence: alert recipient, threshold, and review date
