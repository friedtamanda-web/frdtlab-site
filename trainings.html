The real problem

The gap is not talent. Nobody built the bridge.

A few power users pull ahead. Everyone else stalls. The org quietly splits into people who got faster and people who got left behind, not because of ability, but because no one connected the tool to the workflow. That bridge is the product.

This is not AI literacy, and not a webinar that fades by Friday. The deliverable is not attendance. It is a team that operates differently, plus the trained people, documented systems, and reusable assets that hold the new behavior in place.

Built for privately held, founder-led, and mission-driven organizations that plan to own what they build, and measure leverage in output per person, not headcount added.
